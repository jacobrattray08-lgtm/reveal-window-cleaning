Common questions

Before you book

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as we can get to the windows, we can clean them whether you're in or out.

What if my gate's locked and you can't get round?

We won't charge you for windows we couldn't reach. If we can clean some but not all, that visit is half price — and we'll let you know either way.

Am I tied into a contract?

No. There's no contract and no tie-in. You can stop your cleans whenever you like.

How do I pay?

Direct Debit through Squeegee and GoCardless, or cash on the day — whichever suits you.
