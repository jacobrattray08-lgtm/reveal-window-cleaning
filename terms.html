Who we are

Reveal Window Cleaning, 11 Park Nook, Halifax, HX3 9TT. Phone 07727 864273, email Revealwindowcleaning@hotmail.com.

No contract, no tie-in

There's no minimum term. You can stop your cleans at any time by phone or email — see "Cancelling" below.

Pricing

Prices shown online are per visit, confirmed on your first visit. If your windows haven't been cleaned in a while, the first clean may cost a little more — we'll always agree this with you before we start work.

Your 14-day right to cancel

Booking online creates a distance contract, and you have a statutory 14-day right to cancel from the day the contract is made. If you tick the box at booking asking for your first clean as soon as possible, you're still entitled to cancel within 14 days — but you'll be charged for any clean already carried out. Within that 14-day window, you can cancel by phone or by email; you don't need to call.

Missed visits

If we can't access your property — bad weather, a locked gate, or we simply couldn't attend — you won't be charged, and your next clean goes ahead on your normal cycle. There's no catch-up visit for a missed one. If we clean some windows but can't reach the rest, that visit is charged at half the normal price.

Cancelling

Call or email us and we'll action it for us. We'll confirm your cancellation in writing and cancel any Direct Debit mandate.

Payment

Direct Debit, collected via Squeegee and GoCardless, or cash on the day. Direct Debit collections follow the Direct Debit Guarantee. Cash customers may let a balance build up; we'll always let you know before it becomes a problem.

If you're not happy

Tell us and we'll come back and put it right. Contact us using the details above.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The current version always applies from the date shown at the top of this page.
