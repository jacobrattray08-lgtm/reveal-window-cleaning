Privacy policy

Last updated 25 August 2026.

This policy is a plain-English starting point covering the basics of UK GDPR. Have a solicitor or accountant review it against your actual data practices before you rely on it — see build spec §13.

Who we are

Reveal Window Cleaning, 11 Park Nook, Halifax, HX3 9TT. You can contact us on 07727 864273 or at Revealwindowcleaning@hotmail.com.

What we collect

When you get a price or book with us, we collect your name, address, postcode, phone number, email address, and anything you choose to tell us in the notes field (for example about access to your property). If you book, we also record your chosen frequency, price, and payment preference.

Why we collect it

To give you an accurate price, to arrange and carry out your cleaning visits, to take payment, and to contact you about your service. We do not sell your data.

Where it's stored

Booking and customer records are held in Squeegee, our round management system, and payments are processed through GoCardless. Both are third-party services with their own security and privacy practices.

How long we keep it

For as long as you're a customer, and for a reasonable period afterwards to meet our accounting and legal obligations.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (subject to what we're legally required to keep). Contact us using the details above.

Cookies

This site does not currently set non-essential cookies. If we add analytics or similar tools later, we'll ask for your consent first and update this section.

Questions or concerns

Contact us directly using the details above. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you feel it hasn't been resolved.
