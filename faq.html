FAQ

Questions people usually ask before booking

If something here doesn't answer it, call us on 07727 864273 or email Revealwindowcleaning@hotmail.com.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as we can get to the windows, we can clean them whether you're in or out. If access needs a gate unlocking or a car moving, just let us know in the notes when you book.

How do you reach the upstairs windows?

With a water-fed pole system from ground level, for almost every property. It cleans the glass with purified water and no chemicals, and it's how upstairs and roof windows get cleaned safely without a ladder against your house.

Do you use my water or electricity?

No. We bring our own purified water on the van, so there's nothing to switch on and nothing to pay for.

Why are the windows wet when you leave?

That's the purified water finishing the job. It dries naturally without leaving marks or streaks, so there's no need to towel it off — give it a few minutes and you'll see the difference.

What if my gate's locked and you can't get round?

If we can't reach any windows at all, we won't charge you for that visit and you'll be back on your normal cycle next time. If we can clean some but not the rest, that visit is charged at half the normal price rather than in full.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain doesn't usually stop us — it doesn't undo a clean. In genuinely bad weather we'll skip that visit, you won't be charged, and we'll see you on your normal cycle.

How do I pay?

Direct Debit through Squeegee and GoCardless, or cash on the day — you choose when you book. Direct Debit is collected automatically after each visit; cash can be paid on the day or left to build up, which we'll always let you know about.

Am I tied into a contract?

No. There's no contract and no tie-in. You can stop your cleans whenever you like by phone or email.

How do I stop my cleans?

Call or email us and we'll sort it out for you — there's no cancel button on the site, so a real person always handles it and can put things right if there's a reason to stay.

When will you first come?

We'll confirm your first visit date within 24 hours of booking. We don't currently run fixed weekly days across every area, so we'll agree a date with you directly.

What if I'm not happy with the clean?

Let us know and we'll come back and put it right. We'd always rather hear about it than have you quietly not book us again.

Do you clean the insides too?

Yes, as a separate occasional job — it's not part of the regular exterior round. Book it as a one-off whenever suits you.

Do you do business premises?

Yes. Commercial work is quoted in person rather than online, since access and premises vary too much to price blind. Get in touch and we'll arrange a visit.
